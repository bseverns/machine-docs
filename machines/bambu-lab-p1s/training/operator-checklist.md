# Operator Checklist - Bambu Lab P1S

Trainee:
Trainer:
Date:

## Knowledge

- [ ] Can identify hot surfaces, pinch/motion zones, and safe shutdown path.
- [ ] Can explain why the enclosure is not a substitute for ventilation.
- [ ] Can identify approved starter materials and controlled materials.
- [ ] Can explain why TPU defaults to external spool path.
- [ ] Can explain when full printer calibration is required.
- [ ] Can explain the difference between printer-side calibration and filament/profile calibration.

## Setup

- [ ] Selects P1S, nozzle, plate, filament, and profile correctly in Bambu Studio.
- [ ] Cleans and handles the build plate without contaminating the print surface.
- [ ] Loads filament through the correct AMS or external path.
- [ ] Checks purge waste, nozzle area, and first-layer risk before starting.

## Operation

- [ ] Watches homing, purge, probing, and first layer.
- [ ] Cancels safely when first layer or purge behavior is wrong.
- [ ] Handles finished plates and scrapers safely.
- [ ] Records validation prints, maintenance, and incidents in the correct logs.

## Sign-off

- [ ] Printed a PLA validation cube with trainer present.
- [ ] Logged the run with material, profile, plate, and measurements.
- [ ] Cleared for starter PLA jobs.
- [ ] Cleared for PETG jobs.
- [ ] Cleared for ABS/ASA jobs.
- [ ] Cleared for AMS multi-material jobs.
