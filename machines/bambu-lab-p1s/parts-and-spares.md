## Verify before buying

- Installed nozzle size and material.
- Build plate type(s) in local circulation.
- AMS model, count, hub/buffer configuration, and serials.
- Current Bambu Lab spare-part SKUs and regional availability.
- Whether the lab standardizes on complete hotends or nozzle-only service.

## Upgrade gates

- Hardened nozzle install must be logged before abrasive material approval.
- Any third-party build plate, nozzle, hotend, gear, fan, camera, firmware, or AMS accessory belongs in [`local-deviations.md`](./local-deviations.md).
- Any safety-critical substitute needs maintainer approval and a rollback plan.
