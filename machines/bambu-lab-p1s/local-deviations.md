## Items that must be recorded here

- Hardened nozzle or non-stock nozzle size.
- Third-party hotend, extruder gear, fan, build plate, camera, AMS accessory, riser, duct, or enclosure change.
- Firmware channel changes, beta firmware, LAN-only/cloud policy changes, or account/network restrictions.
- Non-stock Bambu Studio/OrcaSlicer workflow.
- ABS/ASA ventilation workflow that differs from the shared lab standard.
- Any material that requires special drying, filter, chamber, or nozzle controls.
