# Preflight Checklist - Bambu Lab P1S

- [ ] Safety glasses on; hair/sleeves/jewelry controlled.
- [ ] Material is approved for this operator, room, nozzle, and plate.
- [ ] Purge waste/chute clear.
- [ ] Build plate clean, undamaged, and selected correctly in Bambu Studio.
- [ ] Nozzle area clear; silicone sock and front cover seated.
- [ ] Filament dry, labeled, untangled, and routed through approved AMS/external path.
- [ ] Bambu Studio printer model, nozzle size, plate, filament, and profile verified.
- [ ] Slice preview checked for orientation, supports, brim, purge tower, and collision risk.
- [ ] Bed leveling enabled for normal jobs.
- [ ] Full printer calibration completed if moved, serviced, updated, crashed, or drifting.
- [ ] First layer will be observed before leaving the printer.
